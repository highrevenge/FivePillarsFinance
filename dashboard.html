Guide & Definitions

How to read every sheet

Dashboard
Visual overview: KPIs, spending breakdown, 50/30/20 comparison, budget progress — all live from your entries.
Available Cash
Income − Savings − Spending − Investments − Protection Costs.
Income
Expected and actual income; actual income is what feeds the dashboard.
Savings
Goal, target, amount saved, and deadline — Progress % is calculated for you.
Spending
Every expense classified Need or Want; Difference = Budgeted − Actual.
Investments
Contributions and current value; Gain/Loss and Return % are calculated.
Protection
Insurance and other safeguards, including coverage amounts and premiums.
Your data
Kept in this browser only, on this device, so the dashboard stays live between visits until you reset it.
